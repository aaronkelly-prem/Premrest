# 01_spot-concentration — find firms worth a top-down approach

One job: surface firms where Nick Grace has multiple accounts that are going well, so Aaron can reach over the top to a firm-level exec.

## Inputs
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/prospecting-model.md
- Working (this run): Pulse — Nick's accounts, opportunities, service orders, contact activity. Group by parent firm; look for concentration (several accounts, one firm) and traction (active, healthy, recent positive activity).

Do NOT load: the whole _shared folder, other stages' outputs, Firmable (that is stage 02).

## Process
1. Pull Nick's account spread from Pulse and group accounts by their parent firm.
2. Rank firms by concentration (how many accounts) and traction (how well they are going).
3. For each strong candidate, note the firm, the accounts Nick runs there, and the proof-of-traction Aaron can lean on.
4. Keep it to firms that genuinely clear the trigger — a single account is not a trigger.

## Outputs
- candidates-{date}.md → output/  (firm, Nick's accounts there, traction evidence, why now)

## Human check
Aaron reads the shortlist and agrees which firms are worth a top-down partnership approach. He may cut or reprioritise. The chosen firm(s) go to stage 02.
