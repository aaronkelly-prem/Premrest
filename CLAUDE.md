# Premrest — GM Workspace

Aaron Kelly, GM of Cleaning at Premier Restorations Group (Premrest). The single home for everything Premrest: prospecting, ops, calendar, email, pricing, podcast, team, and finishing what gets started. Walled off from all personal and other-business work.

Built on ICM: folders carry sequencing, hierarchy carries context, files carry state. If something needs explaining, it goes in that folder's `CONTEXT.md`, not in anyone's head.
