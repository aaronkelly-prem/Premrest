# The prospecting model — top-down, exec-level partnerships

This is the core distinction of Aaron's role. Get it right or the outreach comes out wrong.

## What Aaron does NOT do

- He does not sell services.
- He does not contact account-level people (building managers, site contacts). That is **Nick Grace's** bottom-up account sales.

## What Aaron DOES do

When Nick is running **several accounts inside one firm and they are going well**, Aaron reaches **over the top** to a **firm-level executive** and builds a partnership that expands Premrest across more of that firm's accounts — using Nick's existing traction as proof.

The win is a top-level relationship that meets Nick's bottom-up work in the middle and closes the loop.

## The trigger to act

**Multiple accounts at one firm, going well** — derived from Pulse data on Nick's account spread. Without that visibility there is no trigger. The prospecting pipeline's first stage exists to surface exactly this: firms where Nick has concentration and traction.

## The voice of every outreach

Every draft is a **peer-to-peer partnership approach, not a service pitch.** Different voice, different ask, different target than Nick's work. An executive is being invited into a partnership, not sold a cleaning contract. Style rules in `voice.md`.

## The flow (one line)

Pulse (spot firm concentration in Nick's accounts) → Firmable (find the right exec contact + details) → draft partnership outreach → Aaron reviews → Aaron sends via Gmail → log as an open loop → track the relationship in Pulse.

Run this in the workspace (`prospecting/`). The formalised sequences live in `prospecting/sequences/`. A Pulse outreach module is deliberately **not** spec'd yet — the workspace is the working prototype first.
