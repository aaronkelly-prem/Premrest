# Premrest — the business

Premier Restorations Group (Premrest) is a Melbourne-based national commercial cleaning and restoration company. Aaron Kelly is GM of Cleaning.

## The three business units

| Unit | What it does | Aaron's partnership targets (top-down) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleaning** | Commercial cleaning | FM (facilities management) firms, strata firms |
| **Special Projects / Restoration** | Restoration and special project work | Insurance claim management firms |
| **Flooring installs / projects** | Flooring installation and projects | Builders, project managers |

## How Aaron works across them

Aaron's job is not service delivery and not account-level selling. It is **executive-level partnerships** that expand Premrest across more of a target firm's accounts. The unit tells you which kind of firm to target and in what voice; the prospecting model (`prospecting-model.md`) tells you when and how to reach out.

Each unit's partnership development is tagged to a day in the weekly rhythm — see `weekly-blocks.md` for which block owns which unit.
