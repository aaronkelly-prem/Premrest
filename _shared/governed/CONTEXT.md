# Governed — staff, HR, and sensitive material

The most sensitive content in the workspace. Employment, hiring, and performance material, and anything drawn from Slack about people. Walled into this folder and governed by the rules below.

## What counts as governed

- Staff performance, conduct, discipline, accountability
- Hiring, candidates, terminations
- 1:1 notes, team issues about specific people
- Anything read live from `premrest.slack.com` about a person or a people-issue

The team roster in `../team.md` is **org context** (who does what), not governed HR content. The line is: *roles and responsibilities* are shared context; *judgments about a person* are governed.

## The rules

1. **Never leaks.** Governed material never surfaces in an outreach draft, a podcast note, anything near LinkedIn or the personal workspaces, or any output outside this folder's purpose. This is a hard extension of the wall in `../tools.md`.
2. **Claude drafts and informs; it never acts.** On staff or HR matters Claude may summarise, draft, and lay out options when asked. It never takes action, sends anything, or raises a people-issue unprompted.
3. **Sensitive by default.** If it is unclear whether something about a person is governed, treat it as governed.
4. **Slack is live reference, on request.** Read the channels Aaron is in to get accurate context when he asks. Not standing monitoring of staff.

## State

No governed content is stored here yet. Slack is read live when Aaron asks. If durable governed notes are ever needed, they live in this folder only, and nowhere else.
